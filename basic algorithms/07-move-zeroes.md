## Problem: Move Zeroes (Easy)

**Link:** [LeetCode Move Zeroes](https://leetcode.com/problems/move-zeroes/)

### Approach

I moved all the non-zero elements to the beginning of the array while keeping their original order. After that, I filled the remaining positions with zeroes.

### Complexity

* Time: O(n)
* Space: O(1)

### Notes

The relative order of the non-zero elements should remain the same. All zeroes must be moved to the end of the array.
